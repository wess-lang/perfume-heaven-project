ABOUT US

Our Story

Founded by Wessam Makkawi, a dedicated Computer Information Systems student at the University of Jordan, Perfume Heaven was born from a passion for the intersection of olfactory art and digital precision. What started as a curated selection has evolved into a premier Online Store for fragrance connoisseurs.

The Philosophy

At our Online Store, we believe that a scent is more than just a fragrance—it is a digital signature of one's personality. By utilizing advanced logic and a deep understanding of aromatic chemistry, we ensure that every bottle in our collection represents the pinnacle of luxury and longevity.

Modern Elegance

We pride ourselves on providing a seamless technological experience. From our secure encrypted checkout to our global sourcing of rare essences, the Wessam Makkawi Online Store remains at the forefront of the modern perfume industry, bridging the gap between traditional hospitality and global digital standards.
